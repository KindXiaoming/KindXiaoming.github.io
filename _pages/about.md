Hi! I'm **Ziming Liu** (刘子鸣). I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in [College of AI](https://collegeai.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/). I am the founder of [MetaCircle](https://meta-circle.com/) (元环智能), whose mission is to uncover **Science of AI** and use this science to achieve **AI for AI**. 

I did my postdoc in AI + Neuroscience at Stanford with Andreas Tolias. I did my PhD in AI + Physics at MIT with Max Tegmark. Before that, I obtained my B.S. in physics from Peking University in 2020. [Get my CV here](/assets/pdf/Ziming_resume.pdf).

I'm broadly interested in the intersection of AI and Science:

* Science of AI: Understanding AI using science. I'm interested in understanding intriguing network phenomena (e.g., grokking, neural scaling laws). For example: [How Do Machines ‘Grok’ Data?](https://www.quantamagazine.org/how-do-machines-grok-data-20240412/).
* Science for AI: Advancing AI using science. When the scaling of the current paradigm plateaus, it is time to focus back on fundamental science (for AI). For example: [The Physical Process That Powers a New Type of Generative AI](https://www.quantamagazine.org/new-physics-inspired-generative-ai-exceeds-expectations-20230919/).
* AI for Science: Advancing science using AI. I'm excited about inventing accurate/interpretable AI4Science models and curiosity-driven AI scientists. For example: [Novel Architecture Makes Neural Networks More Understandable](https://www.quantamagazine.org/novel-architecture-makes-neural-networks-more-understandable-20240911/).
